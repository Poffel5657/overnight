[ WHAT IS THIS? ]

Overnight is a tiny role-playing game that is never finished.

Every night at midnight (Berlin time), the world gets bigger. An AI reads what the players asked for, picks what fits, writes the code, tests it and ships it, all on its own. In the game, nobody has met whoever does this. They call it the Nightwright.

Nobody plans the game in advance. What exists tomorrow depends on what happened today: what you suggested, what you voted for, what the community managed together. Every old version stays playable, so you can watch the world grow from a single meadow.

[ HOW A NIGHT WORKS ]

  1. 00:00 The Nightwright wakes up with no memory. It reads its own notes: the plan, the chronicle, the story so far.
  2. READ It reads the new ideas, counts the votes and the ratings of the last night.
  3. BUILD It builds three things: at least one player idea, the winner of the vote, and one idea of its own that moves the story along. Every seventh night it tidies up instead.
  4. TEST Over a hundred automatic checks make sure nothing broke and old save games still load. If a check fails, the night is not published.
  5. SHIP A few minutes later the new version is live, and a picture of it lands in the timelapse.

[ HOW YOU TAKE PART ]

Suggest
One idea per night for something that should exist tomorrow.
Vote
Old ideas get a second chance. The favourite gets built.
Rate
Tell the Nightwright whether last night was any good. It listens.
Events
Sometimes someone in the world needs help from everyone. Talk to them.
Chat
Anonymous, and the names change every night.

No accounts. Ideas and votes are kept without your name or IP address. To allow one idea, one vote and one chat name per night, a scrambled fingerprint of your connection is kept for about a day.
