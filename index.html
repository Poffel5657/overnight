Overnight

    Timelapse

    Arrow keys or WASD to move. Walk into things to use them. Space to wait.

    Chronicle

    Everything that has ever appeared, and the night it appeared. Every old version stays playable.

      What should exist tomorrow?

      The Nightwright adds to this world every night and reads every idea. It picks what fits, so yours may show up in some form, or not at all.

      One idea per night. Your text is kept without your name or IP address. To enforce the limit, a scrambled fingerprint of your connection is kept for about a day, then deleted. Please don't include personal details.

      Second chances

      Old ideas the Nightwright liked but has not built yet. The one with the most votes gets built at midnight.

      Pick one

      One vote per night. Only your choice is kept, without your name or IP address. To enforce the limit, a scrambled fingerprint of your connection is kept for about a day, then deleted.
